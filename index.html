Privacy Policy

Arcade Rush · Last updated 2 October 2026

This policy explains what information Arcade Rush handles, why, and what choices you have. Arcade Rush is a collection of arcade and learning mini-games for Android and iOS, published by Qalb-E-Ali Syed.

The short version. Arcade Rush has no accounts and no sign-up. Your game progress stays on your device and is never sent to us. We don't collect your name, email, photos, contacts or location. The app shows advertising, and our advertising partner collects limited technical information in order to show those ads. Nothing else leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

Arcade Rush saves the following locally, in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet:

  • Game progress, high scores and statistics
  • Arcade coins and Star Stickers you have earned
  • Cosmetic items you have unlocked
  • Daily challenge and streak progress
  • Your settings, such as sound and music preferences

This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Advertising

Arcade Rush shows advertising through Google AdMob, a service operated by Google. To serve ads, AdMob may collect and process limited technical information, including:

  • Device identifiers, such as an advertising ID
  • Device type, operating system version and similar technical details
  • Approximate, coarse location derived from an IP address
  • Information about ads that were shown, such as views and interactions

Ads in Arcade Rush are non-personalised. Because the app is intended for a family audience that includes children, every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to a general audience rating. We do not use behavioural or interest-based advertising, and we do not permit personalised advertising in this app.

Arcade Rush does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework on iOS and never asks to track you across other apps and websites, because it does not do so.

Google's handling of this information is governed by its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. Information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services is available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Arcade Rush offers one optional in-app purchase, Arcade Premium, which permanently removes advertising. Purchases are processed entirely by the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store, depending on your device.

We never see, receive or store your payment details. The store tells the app only whether the purchase was successful, so that advertising can be switched off. That status is stored on your device and can be restored at any time using the Restore Purchases option in Settings.

Children's privacy

Arcade Rush is designed to be suitable for a family audience, including children. In line with that:

  • The app does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photograph or any other personal detail
  • There are no accounts, logins or profiles
  • There is no chat, messaging, user-generated content or social feature of any kind
  • Advertising is non-personalised and limited to a general audience rating
  • There are no links out of the app to social media or other external sites

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in some way, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Information we do not collect

To be explicit, Arcade Rush does not collect, request or transmit:

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your precise location
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, camera or microphone
  • Any health, financial or biometric information
  • Any account or login credentials

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone other than Google AdMob for the advertising purposes described above. We may disclose information if we are legally required to do so, for example in response to a valid court order.

Your choices

  • Remove advertising entirely by purchasing Arcade Premium. No ad requests are made once it is active.
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. On Android this is under Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS it is under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app. All locally stored data is removed with it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
